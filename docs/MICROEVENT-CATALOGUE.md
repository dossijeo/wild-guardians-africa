# Microevent catalogue and sprite specification

All 40 proposed entries are assembled from shared presentation primitives. New catalogue entries have cosmetic outcomes: no new crop growth, irrigation, currency, worker debuffs, weather, structural damage or topology. The original seed coin and bounded bird-sale adapter remain unchanged. Each entry declares its primary continuous interaction in `src/microevents/interactions.js`. Every entry supports deliberate hold after input ownership as an accessible alternative; see `MICROEVENT-INTERACTIONS.md` for the mechanics and remaining unsupported simulation consequences. Hover alone cannot complete an event.

The original low-poly 3D-rendered transparent 512×512 lossless WebP atlas is 60,476 bytes. Internal animation is restricted to birds, butterflies, locusts, flames and brief lightning. All other movement is particle motion. Regenerate with `node tools/generate_microevent_assets.mjs` and this document with `node tools/document_microevent_catalogue.mjs`.
