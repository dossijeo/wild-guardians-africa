# Buffalo camera tree transition: exploratory comparison

In the native island mangrove combat QA, a camera teleport places the buffalo close to the camera. The conservative frustum-sphere test selects 31 trees, including trees potentially intersecting the image edges. At the first draw, both a 1 s fade and an experimental 0.1 s fade still show incomplete readiness for all 31 trees. At sampled frame 5, all 31 are ready with the shorter fade; with the original fade they remain in transition at frame 15 and are ready by frame 30. Visual time is advanced by a synthetic 1/30 s per draw; these are not real frame times or performance results. Captures include an artificially dense combat fixture and do not prove behavior across biomes or normal camera movement.

Three options merit comparison: prepare the destination view before a hidden loading/reveal transition; maintain relevant native tree resources ahead of ordinary camera movement; or shorten the fade after readiness. The last option was tested here. It does not eliminate the initial impostor and may make the change more abrupt, so it is not adopted in production. Prewarming must be measured for loading, CPU, GPU and memory costs and cannot be assumed available after arbitrary teleports. Readiness is not equivalent to demonstrated pixel visibility. Further tests must include the actual loading/reveal sequence and moving cameras with GPU and frame-time measurements.
