# WebGPU confirmed crops (experimental)

`createWebGpuCropRoster` consumes reconciled crop state in the presentation Worker. It loads the 40 authored crop meshes when the first living crop is confirmed, then requests the original 32 baked V4 transition templates only when at least one living crop is immature, groups living plants by species and stage or bridge, and shares geometry/material through instancing. The two-second simulation-time transition window, original regional roots and pairings, and growth/wind position and normal deformations are evaluated using TSL. Native terrain is sampled and a crop remains hidden until its chunk is available. The Worker does not own crop simulation or logic.

`tests/webgpu-crop-roster.test.js` checks germination, maturity, the two-second bridge and deferred asset loading. `tools/experiments/audit_webgpu_crop_roster.mjs` loads a real millet plant from the mangrove raid fixture in an OffscreenCanvas Worker, draws the compiled bridge with WebGPU validation enabled, switches stages and checks removal. This is a functional smoke test, not a gameplay, frame-time or complete visual-parity test.

The GPU test does not yet compare every V4 bridge and transition endpoint against the original renderer. Blessings, damage, selection and AfricanToon are not implemented; wind time changes only on confirmed publications rather than an autonomous simulation clock, and height uses a center terrain sample. The bridge asset loads asynchronously; until it is ready, the prior midpoint switch remains as a temporary fallback. The additional asset is approximately 26 MB in the current compressed web package, with initialization and memory costs still unmeasured. Do not enable this experimental view as a production replacement until these gaps are resolved and validated.
