# Native worker budget accounting

WorkerHit and WorkerIncapacitated are mutually exclusive outcomes of the same native decrement in encounters.js. Both spend exactly one animal hit. The observer previously counted only WorkerHit, so its unused budget could be overstated. This change counts the second consumer separately at raid/species level and in legacy observation-window reconstruction. No simulation, damage, seed, command or RNG changes.

18 directed tests passed, including actual physical overlap, a second native encounter after leaving the contact range, save/reload, and an unknown actor rejected as incomplete. The overlap fixture configures a worker near the spawned animal for a consumer unit test; it is not a campaign or proof of ordinary player placement. Observer.report leaves the serialized state unchanged.

The day58 stopped campaign lacks final snapshot/observer data. This static omission cannot establish its exact discrepancy or recover lost evidence. Existing reports remain unchanged. Counting a hit spent on a worker does not imply a crop was harmed.
