# Frozen military baseline: first terminal campaign

The good-management Sabana/Mapungubwe campaign, seed 123, ended in a native economic defeat after 31 completed nights. It was requested for 100 nights and was allowed to continue until the motor declared defeat; it was not stopped for economic fragility. The completed recovery-funding correction was present and the player policy remained frozen.

The original launch was at `2026-10-11T13:58:32.456Z`; the terminal receipt was written at `2026-10-11T15:53:13.835Z`. These elapsed times include concurrent QA and are not an isolated performance benchmark.

The center remained intact at 138 HP, but the farm had no living plants and only 19 coins, below the unchanged recovery requirement. This is neither a technical timeout nor a victory.

The first cohort observer rejected the native report's textual seed and the final receipt's absent options, despite successful terminal audit. Its reader now accepts only the numeric seed or its canonical decimal text and requires the requested 100-night scope in the immutable launch provenance. Contradictory receipt options, wrong seeds, missing/short launch horizons and changed policies still reject a case. No raw campaign evidence was modified; the initial rejection remains preserved.

All 1,555 assigned animal strikes were consumed; unused strike budget was zero. There were 122 plants destroyed. The weighted post-introduction crop loss was 4.252% of the summed pre-incursion censuses. This is a repeated-night exposure measure, not the fraction of distinct plants lost, nor proof of a causal wall advantage without paired strategies.

The daily evidence also shows stock contraction during nights without crop destruction. For example, nights 19–24 recorded no destroyed plants; salaries and paid repairs still consumed capital. Calibration must therefore inspect structural attack expenditure and replacement funding as well as agricultural attack damage. This observation does not authorize a new player-policy change, altered wages, cheaper repairs or changed crop economics.

The harness reported 79.871% unoccupied time. This does not meet the below-25% requirement, and is not physical acceptance of a human player's activity. Neither useful activity nor a 100-night win is claimed.
