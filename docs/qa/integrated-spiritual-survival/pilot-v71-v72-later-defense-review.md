All paired source, terminal, delivery/ledger, worker and plant-census audits

Passive day 8 pays 300 wages and 1100 in wall construction against 1309
delivered income, buying no seeds. The field is harvested to zero. Day 9 starts
with 321, pays a one-worker 30-coin native contract, 180 in more walls and 77
in real repairs; it again buys no seeds and ends with 34. The defensive plan
is still incomplete and reserves 160 for future unbuilt pieces.

That remaining quote is an automated player saving preference, not a native
debt or automatic maintenance charge. Combined with construction-first buying,
it starves replanting even when physical placement and a native contract are
available. Delaying the whole-perimeter commitment therefore does not resolve
this policy problem. Do not interpret it as proof that magic is mandatory or
adjust damage/prices to compensate.
