## What the extensions establish

Seed 2026's active farm closed its first recorded contour on day 7 at approximately 246 simulated seconds. No center attacks were recorded. Its actual agricultural HP loss was 142 against an agricultural potential of 700. Repairs cost 854 against the advisory curve's 2,505.426 coins; the curve includes introductory nights and is not a fee or a literal calibration requirement. From day 7 to day 14, stock changed from 191 to 196 and cash from 615 to 597. This is short survival with meaningful physical interception and paid maintenance, not evidence of 100-night sustainability.

Early passive defense closed a contour on day 7 at approximately 294 seconds, but that historical closure is not a guarantee that subsequent expansion remains protected. Its final pending larger contour still had a native quote of 320 coins. The opening day spent 240 coins on walls and 180 on six workers; day 2 planted nothing while renewing six workers. Stock fell to five on day 9, stayed at five on day 10, and the remaining crops were lost in night 11. On day 11, 77 coins of delivered income funded 30 in wages, 35 in seeds and 27 in repairs; closing balance was 30. The center survived. The terminal snapshot and event order prove dawn irreversibility after a fully completed raid, rather than technical timeout or center destruction.

This failed passive policy is not proof that agricultural magic must be compulsory: early defense timing, workforce renewal and replacement funding still need separate investigation. It must not be corrected by free repairs, invented harvests, altered prices or silent removal of enemies. The active/passive columns differ in seed and wall start day and are not a controlled estimate of magic's causal effect.

The decision-window idle proxy is not measured human activity. Neither campaign approves the under-25% manual-inactivity requirement, GPU performance, browser QA or main integration. Retain negative results and test coherent short policies before launching 100/180-day campaigns. Seed 123's fourteen-night extension (v86) remained live at this review; its seven recorded rows do not constitute a terminal result.
