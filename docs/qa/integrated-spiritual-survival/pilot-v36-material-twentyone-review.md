# Native material comparison: seed 2026, 21 nights

Both good-management campaigns complete 21 nights with verified native delivery,
raid and ledger evidence. They share frozen simulation source hashes and initial
seed, biome/culture, Q9 labor, cashflow crops, shore perimeter, day-6 construction
and 1.5 clearance. Only the selected defensive material changes. Native prices,
damage, magic budgets and agricultural rules are unchanged.

Both final centers retain 600 HP. No entry failures or observer coverage loss
occur. Exact cash reconciliation starts from 700 after the paid original center.
The terminal census independently reconciles actual plant placements, pickups,
destruction and physical crates. No income, repairs or loss fractions are injected.

Empalizada spends 801 more on defense, ends with 267 more cash and 74 more living
crops. It initially sacrifices planting: day 6 buys one crop versus 77 with zarzas,
because its perimeter costs twice as much. It then recovers. At day 14 it has
200 living crops and 562 coins; at day 21 it has 175 and 950. Productive inventory
still declines by 25 in the final week, versus 91 with zarzas. Thus this is
promising protection evidence, not acceptance of 100-night sustainability.

The reduction of 286 cumulative destroyed plants is an observed policy outcome,
not a guaranteed defensive multiplier. Crop exposure, pressure, composition and
RNG consumption evolve differently after initial spending diverges. Strike counts
are consequently different; the experiment does not claim identical attacks.
Do not change attack parameters to imitate this result or silently switch all
historical controls to the stronger material.

Retained evidence: both original campaigns, material terminal audit, population
census and comparison CSV/JSON/PNG/SVG. The plotted chart was inspected; this is
chart QA, not a GPU benchmark. Manual inactivity below 25%, other seeds/cultures,
long survival and postgame expansion remain unverified. Repeat this material
policy on another seed before approving long campaigns.
