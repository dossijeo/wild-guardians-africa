# Passive recovery control: native defeat after night 12

The requested fourteen-night campaign terminated normally in native defeat
at the dawn following its twelfth completed night. Seed 712,
Savanna/Mapungubwe, elder women, Q12 funding, cashflow crops, shore wooden
defenses from day 6, breach-first repairs, 1.5 fluid preference, no agricultural
magic and native Shield. It is not a transport failure or a fourteen-night
completion.

All historical v64 runtime hashes match. The first seven complete daily rows
match v64 exactly. Only the opted-in QA labour policy differs. Its empty-field
forecast paid three workers 90 coins on day 8 and bought 40 crops through real
commands. That demonstrates initial recovery, not sustainable economics.

Final center health is 600. Only fifteen crops were destroyed, all on night 6.
All 271 assigned strikes were consumed. Subsequent walls intercepted attacks
and incurred real repair payments. Terminal, plant census, worker contract and
delivery-ledger audits pass.

Exact post-opening reconciliation:

`700 + 4618 delivered income - 1763 wages - 2169 seeds - 1000 walls - 355 repairs = 31`.

The dawn-12 contract spent 60 out of 62 coins on two workers, leaving 2 coins
and a protected next-payroll reserve of 60. On dawn 11 the same crew spent
60 out of 91, leaving 31 with that same 60 reserve. The Q12 helper anticipates
replanting only for an empty field; its nonempty-field renewal still accepts
current-payable crews with unfunded working capital. Thus this is a legitimate
native defeat under the automated decisions, but it does not establish that a
responsible passive player must lose or needs agricultural magic to survive.

Before calibrating military intensity from this strategy, examine funded crew
renewal and the reservation of all future, unbuilt wall costs. Do not lower
wages, alter native crop production, waive repairs or grant forecast income.
Any revised QA strategy must use actual paid hiring and purchases, be frozen
as a new candidate, and retain this failed campaign unchanged.

defensive investment, temporary replanting and shrinking field. Its activity
proxy is not human manual time and does not meet the under-25% acceptance.
No production merge or hundred-night acceptance follows from this pilot.
