# Candidate three: completed seed-712 native short comparison

All three campaigns are terminal native evidence with identical imported-source hashes. The protected strategy completed 21 nights; the bad strategy reached native defeat after 17 completed nights; the productive no-wall/no-shield control completed 21 nights. No hundred-night run is approved from these results.

The protection is physically measurable: 740 impacts hit walls, which required 740 coins of construction and 1,908 of paid repair/reconstruction. There were 218 completed repair actions and 166 structure-ruin events (repeated destruction of rebuilt pieces is possible). The protected campaign lost 579 fewer plants than the unprotected control, approximately 75.3% fewer cumulative losses. This is an observed comparison between whole strategies, including shield use and different resulting economies; it is not a guaranteed wall-only reduction or a matched-census loss-rate experiment.

The cheapest `zarzas` perimeter is not demonstrated to be economically optimal. Relative to the unprotected strategy, protection reduced seed spending by 5,580 and wages by 2,400, but delivered 5,683 less income and spent 2,648 on defenses. Those differences reconcile exactly to its 351 lower final cash. This warrants investigating labor allocation, production disrupted by repair tasks and defensive design/materials before treating the protected bot as optimal or raising military pressure indiscriminately.

Neither surviving campaign shows explosive cash accumulation in the last seven days: protected delivered 10,526 and spent 10,349 (+177); unprotected delivered 13,796 and spent 13,664 (+132). Survival without walls remains possible at this short horizon. The native bad-policy defeat establishes a real loss outcome, but does not by itself establish that neglecting defense will make an otherwise productive farm insolvent later.

All daily financial ledgers reconcile exactly. Every assigned animal hit was accounted for and every recorded retirement had zero hits remaining. The corrected observer includes four worker incapacitations in the protected campaign; the prior fourteen-day campaign's finance, cash, living, destruction, planting and deliveries remain exactly equal for all fourteen days. Historical observer omissions and original reports are preserved.

The comparison chart was opened and inspected. Daily CSV, chart JSON, original snapshots, source hashes, protocol and terminal receipt are retained. Activity figures remain automated decision-window proxies; human manual activity has not been measured and the under-25% inactivity requirement is not accepted. Canyon paid-defense certification, the other strategies/seeds, long campaigns, postgame expansion and runtime visual/GPU QA remain outstanding.

Next calibration should first improve or diagnose real paid defense and labor policy, preserve this candidate as a frozen baseline and use short comparable pilots. No prices, target reservations or damage parameters were changed to produce this report.
