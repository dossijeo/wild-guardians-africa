# Explicit paid defense material experiment

The native campaign CLI accepts `--defense-material` for funded/routed/shore policies. The default remains `zarzas`; an explicit `empalizada` choice selects the existing material with its unchanged native price (20 coins), HP (200; gate 120), geometry, placement checks, collisions and repair rules. No money, HP, production, threat or damage is granted or altered by this option.

Material is recorded in the campaign protocol, receipt options, final policy and funded-defense history. Actual partial purchases and worker repairs still use native commands and ledger settlement. Unaffordable construction must remain incomplete rather than becoming a fictional complete perimeter. Invalid materials or unsupported policies are rejected.

The comparison tool normally rejects differing materials. `--vary-defense-material` explicitly permits a single-factor material experiment only while preserving the strategy and all other already checked settings and exact imported-source hashes. Lines and CSV rows identify the material. This does not imply equivalent final planting, workforce or geometry: those can diverge through real player budgets and decisions, and must be reported.

First pilot plan: seven native nights, seed 712, Sabana/Mapungubwe, `good`, Q8 labor, cashflow crop choice, shore defense starting day 6, fluid clearance 1.5. Run zarzas and empalizada from the same frozen source. Check the default zarzas daily finance and native losses against the historical seven-day baseline before attributing differences to wood.

Do not promote a material merely because it has more HP. Measure actual funded coverage, wall/crop contacts, destruction, repairs, deliveries, wages, cash and crop losses. If the expensive option cannot close its perimeter or clearly harms recovery, retain the negative evidence and stop escalating it. These short experiments do not approve hundred-night balance, human inactivity or production integration.
