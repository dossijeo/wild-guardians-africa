# Positive service cache: native scaling pilot

`pilot-v122-positive-service-cache-good-sabana-123-fourteen/` ran the same frozen Q15 good Sabana / Mapungubwe / seed 123 campaign, cashflow crops, day-six shore defense, breach-first repairs and rolling defense funds. The runtime candidate is commit `60ff0167`. No balance or player-strategy parameters changed. Its first nine daily JSON rows are byte-for-byte identical to the pre-cache v121 run.

The campaign completed nights 10, 11 and 12, which previous scaling attempts could not reach at acceptable cost. At night 10 it had 188 living crops, 808 coins, 115 planted and 109 delivered; at night 12 it had 165 living crops and 892 coins. It reached the end of night 12 at 18:52:37 UTC, about 6m26s after starting at 18:46:11 UTC. By comparison, the pre-cache v121 attempt had reached only day-ten time 400 s when stopped after about eight minutes. Host scheduling and CPU conditions differ, so this is evidence of meaningful progress, not a controlled throughput ratio.

Day 13 again became expensive. After a cooperative stop request it eventually saved at day 13 time 390 s, during a raid, with 165 living crops, 757 coins, 27 workers, 153 pending tasks and 1,147 historical crop records. The run finished at 18:58:27 UTC with receipt status `stopped-early-calibration`, 12 complete rows and no observed defeat. The compressed partial state SHA-256 is `1042E7DDABB89A69A4899E648C6F959633CEA35A2014B22E4D56CFE512DA745E`. A 20-s isolated replay from that late raid state cost 1.74 s wall time, but does not recreate the preceding expensive segment; a future checkpoint before that segment is required for attribution.

Focused native service/defense/planning tests passed 21/21; additional canyon and perimeter regression tests passed 15/15; the production web build succeeded. The service cache retains only positive certificates scoped to the actual geometry, target and origin; an identical retained proof returned the original result and reduced a repeated call from 2,615 ms to 4.45 ms. Full campaign performance remains unapproved for 100 nights because day 13 still scales poorly. Raw inactivity fields are historical telemetry and are not an acceptance criterion.
