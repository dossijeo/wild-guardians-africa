# Joint raid-capacity preflight — no runtime change

Nine finite variants were evaluated at the nine completed candidate-2 native undefended encounters, nights 6–14, seed 712 / Sabana / Mapungubwe. The tool reuses the actual pressure, unlocks, crop census and canonical reference geometry. It changes hypothetical count and hit progression only in pure cloned planning inputs. No RNG is consumed; no animal moves, crop dies, ledger changes or agricultural price changes. The 81 rows reproduce byte-for-byte on a second run. Two evidence-integrity tests pass.

Candidate count: round(4 + 30 * P^countPower). Non-baseline variants use a post-introduction warm-up: min(rawCount, 8 + 2 * (night - 6)), starting only after the five unchanged single-species tutorial nights. Hypothetical additional native hit steps: floor(hitSteps * P), replacing floor(2 * P). Full per-species original min/max ranges remain the base. Q stays at 1.5 times the joint reference mean, with the native full-range feasibility safeguard. Species weights, central damage, radius, area cap, obstacle/Shield rules and structural damage per impact are unchanged.

The power=1 / steps=2 baseline has no warm-up change and exactly reproduces the recorded composition and Q. Other variants deliberately include the warm-up; the table is not an isolated single-parameter causal experiment.

The optimistic screen uses maximum legal hit rolls, maximum configured plant count, perfect contacts and free kills for already wounded crops. It differs from the previous diagnostic based on the actually assigned random hit budgets. The baseline can pass this looser screen in three encounters while none of the observed, assigned-budget bounds meets the curve. Passing either bound does not demonstrate physical reachability or a native destruction rate. Mean reference HP is a directional geometric planning quantity, not realized damage or a casualty prediction.

## Selected for the next native pilot, not approved for production

Use countPower=0.5 and hitSteps=4 with the described warm-up. At the recorded night-14 pressure it gives 21 bodies and 129.5 mean reference HP, versus 14 and 68.5 for candidate 2. Structure capacity rises by about 92%; native wall interception, repairs and collapse must be measured before accepting it. Steps=6 gives the same mean at that particular pressure but would add unnecessary late-game hit escalation, so it is not selected merely to clear more optimistic screens. Neither selection guarantees matching the orientative loss curve.

The first five native introductions remain unchanged. Subsequent body counts start at at most eight and can increase by at most two per night until the pressure-derived count is reached. The count formula still reaches 34 at P=1; the active-wave limit remains 16, and every budgeted actor must enter and depart physically. This is a calibration formula, not a new artificial farm-size cap.

Next implementation must version the complete count and hit recipe, not just Q. Save validation needs historical candidate-1 and candidate-2 count/hit formulas; their already committed nights, actors, RNG, EMA and ledgers cannot be regenerated. Capture genuine candidate-2 planned and active-wave fixtures before editing runtime. Only after functional, persistence and wave tests should new short native campaigns run with frozen parameters. Keep negative candidate-2 results, original prices and below-25% activity acceptance requirements. No main merge, 100-night acceptance or postgame acceptance is implied.
