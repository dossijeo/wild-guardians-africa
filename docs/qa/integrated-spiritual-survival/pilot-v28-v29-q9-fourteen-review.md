## Changed interpretation of historical defeats

Protected seed2026 has exactly identical daily rows and complete raid evidence
under Q8 and Q9. Its workload remains large enough to retain the productive crew.

Unprotected seed2026 first downsizes from17 to13 workers on day12, then to10 on
day13. This saves540 in wages versus Q8, purchases365 more seeds, earns77 less
income and finishes with98 more coins:403 rather than305. It replants enough
that the last incursion attacks actual crops instead of an empty farm's center.
The night14 census is69, of which59 are destroyed; ten survive. The prior Q8
version instead harvested its final ten crops, could not fund new planting
while reserving its large crew, and lost its center that night.

Unprotected seed712 also survives night14 under Q9, unlike historical Q8.
Historical defeats therefore cannot be presented as proof that responsibly
adaptive farms necessarily fail by fourteen nights. They were real native
defeats, but the policy's excessive renewal amplified the physical losses.

This does not remove the defensive advantage. The protected seed2026 farm
retains192 crops while the unprotected one retains10, with134 versus557
cumulative crops destroyed. Nevertheless, short survival and population
differences do not prove the hundred-night economy or irreversible decline.
Do not tune damage to force a return to the earlier defeat dates.
