# Q11: isolate an excessively restrictive planting reserve

Native `ensurePurchaseBudget` protects the final 30 coins. The QA Q6–Q10 planting policy additionally requires the whole selected crew's next daily wage before buying any seed. Those rules differ. In the audited Q10 passive pilot, dawn-nine renewal pays 90 coins from 136, leaving 46 coins: a player could natively buy three mijo seedlings and retain 31 coins, while Q10 waits for more delivered income before any planting. The Q10 campaign later pays substantial repairs without replacement planting and defeats. That is a reason to test the policy, not permission to change game prices or declare the military balance correct.

Q11 retains Q10 dawn crew decisions, actual wages, original crop prices/values, repair reservations and physical-delivery-gated additional hiring. It changes **only the labour cash reserve used by planting decisions**. Up to simulated second 140 it protects the native minimum 30 coins; from 140 to the 300-second shift end it linearly increases the target to the whole selected crew's next wage, rounded up. Repairs, walls, expansion and additional hires retain their existing full-renewal gates. This means Q11 still accepts a real financing risk, which must be measured. It is not a guaranteed recovery rule.

The 140-second ramp start is a disclosed experimental early-reinvestment window, corresponding to the ordinary mijo growth duration. It does not advance growth, grant future income or treat a maturity forecast as delivered cash. The target depends only on the current simulated clock and already selected crew; it consumes no RNG and does not reset via save/load. All purchase commands remain subject to native collision, planting and budget checks. There is no planting quota or artificial capacity increase.

Four focused tests cover the monotonic rounded reserve, actual native early planting after a paid three-worker contract, preservation of the final 30 coins, pending repair commitments, restored-clock calculation and explicit CLI/provenance selection. Together with Q9/Q10/CLI regressions, twenty tests pass. The command fixture's initial funds and flat terrain are test inputs, not campaign evidence. Full crew renewal remains separately reported, with planting reserve telemetry distinct.
