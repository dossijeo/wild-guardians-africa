# Native defensive contact and actual repair expenditure

The read-only `tools/audit-native-defense-costs.mjs` reports actual raid contacts/HP loss and paid repair ledger entries. It does not simulate new attacks, assign losses, request repairs or charge maintenance. Source campaigns remain untouched. Duplicate repair payments and mismatched repair subtotals are rejected. All raid strike budgets retain their native consumed/unused split.

Two retained observations illustrate why closing the perimeter must precede military recalibration:

| Retained campaign | Observed nights | First recorded complete contour | Wall contacts | Wall HP lost | Crop losses | Actual repair coins | Advisory curve sum |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v68, good/contour/empalizada | 21 | Day 7, 156 s | 671 | 15,823 | 26 | 1,553 | 1,629.717 |
| v76, good/rolling/empalizada | 14 | Never | 0 | 0 | 367 | 0 | 1,783.407 |

The curve is the user's advisory `12 + 0.42 N + 0.003 N²`. For disclosure the audit chooses `N` from each day's first native **night-spawn living census**, including introductory nights; it never sums repeated exposure as a bigger plant census. Costs are paid on the actual repair day and may concern damage from an earlier night. Agreement of cumulative expenditure with an advisory total is not a pointwise fit or proof of final economic equilibrium. The curve is never charged to the player.

These campaigns differ in funding policy, duration, agricultural census and resulting native RNG history. This is not a matched causal experiment of material strength or an assurance that every wall design is effective. The closed contour's 671 wall contacts and 15,823 actual wall HP loss nevertheless demonstrate real physical interception and paid maintenance. In v76 there was no recorded complete contour and no physical wall contact: raising damage to compensate for this geometry would attack the wrong cause.

Potential agricultural HP totals are 890 and 532; effective agricultural HP totals are 26 and 367. Alternative structural potential is not added to agricultural potential as if both occurred. Native contacts, lost plants, prices and delivered money remain separate quantities.

The fourteen-night zarzas pilots are still running when this review is written. No result from a running receipt is promoted to terminal acceptance. A useful next result must show native closure, contacts, repairs and production stability together. There is still no hundred-night, passive-policy viability, postgame, human inactivity or broad-biome acceptance.

Evidence: `pilot-v68-defense-cost-audit.json` and `pilot-v76-defense-cost-audit.json`; each points to its original terminal report and retains day-by-day raid/cost facts.
