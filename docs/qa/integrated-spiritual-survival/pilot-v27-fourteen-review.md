## Important limitation: adaptive labor recovery remains unproven

The no-shield campaign had 35 crops at the end of day 12 and 10 at the end of
day 13. Its q8 policy nevertheless rehired 17 older workers on day 14, spending
510 of 653 coins. The remaining 143 did not meet that crew's 510 renewal reserve,
so the bot purchased zero new crops. It delivered the remaining ten crops for
162 coins during the day. Those crops were harvested, not destroyed: day 14
records zero crop destruction, and the last raid's initial exposure was zero.
The raid then physically collapsed the last center. Some actors legitimately
retired with 34 total unused strikes after no reachable targets remained.

This is a real native defeat and a measurable cumulative physical disadvantage,
but it is not proof that a responsibly adaptive unprotected policy cannot
recover. `createQ6LabourPolicy.dawn`, inherited by q8, renews the previous crew
whenever any living crop or pending task remains. It does not reduce staffing
after severe loss of workload. Audit this QA decision before definitive
calibration; do not compensate it with seed prices, crop income or animal damage.
Preserve q8 and these negative outcomes when testing a separate candidate.
