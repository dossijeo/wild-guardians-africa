# Incremental funded Canyon policy: certification regression

The integrated policy was tested on the original paid eight-seedling/one-worker Canyon opening with 630 coins and no walls. Each decision uses ordinary commands, a protected 30-coin reserve and native five-second worker advances. The maximum functional observation is 16 decisions, not a farming limit or a campaign strategy quota.

## Preserved failures

V1 spends 310 coins in four native purchases, creates 31 pieces and one gate, and declares slot completion at second 25. Its independent actual proof rejects a centre service pose with `native-service-origin-unproven`. This was a false certification by the QA policy, not an economic defeat or a proved physical escape. The previous policy allowed complete slot coverage to bypass contact/component checks.

The policy now runs the native proof even with complete slot coverage when obstacle-aware mode is active, before approving proposed geometry and when checking the actual paid result. V2 no longer declares unsupported completion. V3 records an independently valid final geometry but still no policy completion. Investigation identifies inconsistent outline passing: rectangular candidates receive their outline during quote proof but not during coverage proof. V4 disables cross-call view caching as a diagnostic; it does not fix the failure. That change is reverted, rather than claiming an unsupported cache defect.

V5 passes the contour consistently for every candidate and records explicit coverage diagnostics. V6 repeats with the original view cache behavior restored. Both finish successfully at second 25. Exact earlier policy and diagnostic-wrapper source bytes are archived and verified against their recorded SHA256 hashes. Raw reports/states V1–V6 are preserved.

The final policy chooses a rectangular candidate rather than the initially selected shore contour. All final animal/service and worker/crop checks pass with actual paid geometry. Ledger debits, owned pieces, native suppression receipts and save/reload are checked. Gates need a separate topology review: two may serve distinct physical faces, but this result does not prove compliance with the one-door-per-enclosure requirement. Do not approve the integration or launch acceptance campaigns until that is resolved.

A new regression exercises real incremental commands and requires an actual certificate at completion, the full candidate outline and zero remaining quote pieces/updates. The first test draft incorrectly assumed the final V3 geometry was still unproved; it failed because that independent geometry was valid while policy coverage omitted its outline. The corrected test captures the actual inconsistency, rather than weakening the certificate.

This is offline QA policy integration. It does not change production, prices, damage, crop resistance or the frozen v105 campaign. Native night attacks and maintenance already validated on the earlier single-gate fixture cannot automatically be attributed to this different incremental two-gate geometry.
