# First frozen Q15 three-strategy comparison, Sabana/Mapungubwe seed 123

This is a **negative native balance result**, not a 100-night success. The 100-night good-management run ended in native defeat during night 36 (35 completed nights). The separately retained `no-walls` control lost during night 19 (18 completed); `bad` lost at dawn after 12 completed nights. All three runs use identical simulation source hashes, agricultural prices, crop health, raid candidate v3, seed, biome/culture, Q15 labour policy and `cashflow` crop policy. Good/no-walls also use the same moderate agricultural magic and shield. The no-walls and bad policies intentionally omit walls; bad additionally makes poorer hiring/repair/magic choices. Good versus no-walls has different named cash/expansion flags, but Q15 supplies the pre-victory labour reserve, village savings are zero before postgame, and neither run bought another centre or village in their shared 18-day prefix. This single-seed comparison is still insufficient for win-rate claims.

The good run's 36/36 raids ended, raid observer status is `verified` with no issues, and every daily ledger reconciliation difference is zero. It paid 820 coins for 82 wall pieces and 3,592 coins for repairs; total delivered-crate income was 27,575, wages 12,943, seeds 10,893. Until night 34, only 27 crops had been destroyed, all on night 6 before the defense was established. The physically protective perimeter therefore works, but accumulated wall wear eventually consumes working capital. The native night-35 breach destroyed 23 crops and the work centre collapsed on night 36. This is not evidence of a pricing, wage, crop-health, magic or simulation-policy change; none were made.

From nights 18–32, the good run lost roughly 1,000–1,900 wall HP each night while paying about 78–206 coins/day in repairs when funds allowed. Agricultural pressure actually declined from about 0.25 to 0.19 as farm value fell, and the animal count fell from 19 to 17. The assault remained capable of inflicting roughly 1,000 wall HP/night; pressure feedback did not halt the attrition spiral. Across the run, warthogs performed 1,209 of the 1,520 species-attributed structure contacts. This identifies **structure damage from weak, numerous animals** as the first military parameter to test. Crop damage/radius, economy, wall HP/cost and virtual-player policies stay frozen. Any candidate must be a new raid recipe version so saved v1–v3 raid plans retain their original damage on reload.

The bad run's raid observer is also `verified`, 12/12 raids ended, no observer issues, and every daily ledger reconciliation difference is zero. Its centre remained intact at terminal defeat, so it is an economic failure, not a fabricated structural collapse.
