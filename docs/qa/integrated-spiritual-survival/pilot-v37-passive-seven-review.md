# Passive farming: native seven-night control, seed 712

The passive Q9 campaign completes seven nights with 116 live crops, 212 coins,
an intact 600 HP work center and no agricultural losses. It uses no agricultural
magic; the seven SpellActivated events are defensive shields. All income comes
from 332 observed physical crate deliveries. Native damage, wages, seeds, wall
construction and repair settlements remain enabled.

Opening cash is 700 after the paid 800-coin center. Both ledgers reconcile exactly.
The 2,070-coin income difference is not entirely a direct magic bonus: 663 coins
are observed Multiplicar receipts; the remaining difference includes deliveries,
crop composition and state-dependent productive decisions. No income is credited
before delivery. Passive census reconciles 448 lifetime placed plants (including
the initial plant), minus 332 physically picked plants, to 116 living plants.

Source hashes, seed, biome/culture, labor policy, crop policy, defensive material,
construction start and clearance match. Strategies differ only in agricultural
magic mode in their configured decision policies. State trajectories, later
pressure and RNG consumption can consequently diverge. The comparison uses seven
actual daily records from each source, explicitly preserving the good campaign's
full 21-night report rather than fabricating a truncated campaign.

This is evidence that the initial seven-night phase can survive without manual
agricultural magic under this policy and seed. It does not establish passive
survival through 100 nights or prove the intervention is unnecessary later.
The passive decision-window idle proxy is 77.95%; neither it nor CPU selection
timings measure human activity duration. The below-25% human inactivity criterion
remains unverified. Magic selection and effect duration receive zero time credit.

Retained evidence: original six campaign files, terminal ledger/delivery audit,
plant census audit and equal-horizon comparison JSON/PNG/SVG. The chart was
visually inspected for readable axes and correct seven-day coverage. This is
chart QA, not rendered-game or GPU acceptance. Continue with a 14-night passive
pilot before considering a long campaign; keep production rules frozen.
