The cash difference reconciles as +402 lower wages +1,546 lower seed expenditure −418 lower income −1,473 defensive expenditure = +57. This compares evolving policies/farms; it is not a randomized wall-only multiplier at identical crop positions. The farms affect subsequent threat pressure through their actual crop values, as specified.

Destroyed crops divided by cumulative per-raid starting exposures is 21.36% for Shield-only versus 4.58% for protected, including introductions. These descriptive aggregates are not guaranteed per-night loss rates. The control without Shield dies from actual center destruction, whereas Shield-only preserves enough targets to survive this horizon. Continue longer short pilots before assuming that either policy can survive 100 nights.
