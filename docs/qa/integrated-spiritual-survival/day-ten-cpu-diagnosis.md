Of all samples, 67.4% are in `native-funded-defense-policy.act`; its native `quote` and `coverage` checks account for about 38.9% and 25.2% respectively. These are rankings under heavy profiling overhead, not uninstrumented durations.

The policy had already deferred repeated quotes for a *completed* paid contour when another piece was unaffordable. The new technical candidate extends the same deferment to an *incomplete planned* contour. Native repairs remain first. Once the protected balance funds a piece again, it performs the full native quote and physical proof before any paid wall command. This avoids a costly proof that cannot authorize a purchase. The focused defense tests include a partial-purchase, unfunded interval and later funded resumption; all 13 pass. It changes no economy, crop, magic, raid or player strategy parameter. A fresh uninstrumented campaign is required to check exact decisions and measure the benefit.
