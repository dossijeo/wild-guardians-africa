# Frozen native policy candidate v6

This changes QA player decisions only, never production placement, seeds,
income, magic budgets, wages, animals, damage or terrain. Candidate v5 and all
its failures remain retained. Do not compare a new v6 policy to an old v5 policy
as though their planting decisions were identical.

`--plot-fluid-clearance 1.5` asks the player strategy to prefer plots whose
16-sided 1.5 m neighbourhood has no water/lava according to the native sampled
fluid-footprint query. Production still allows every originally legal plot.
Every chosen plot must also satisfy original placement and worker return/outward
navigation checks. Search retains finite camera-resident regions and expands
through existing regions; no farm-size or purchase quota is added. The original
CLI default is 0, preserving historical policies. Reused plots recheck fluids,
actual walls and routes. Rejections do not manufacture productive activity.

A native Gran Cañón/Saheliana seed-712 test buys sixty millet plants, pays a
real 30-coin worker contract, and remains at elapsed zero with 370 coins. No
income, time advance or fabricated harvesting finances those purchases.

`--defense-policy shore` extends the existing routed/funded QA policy with a
bounded deterministic shoreline envelope at paddings 2, 3 and 4 m, after the
existing alternatives fail. The contour is proposed only. Quotes still reject
full-water pieces, crops and solid obstacles. Incomplete slot coverage requires
the existing native animal-component and worker-route proof; an outline itself
never counts as protection. Only paid native build commands create walls.
Repairs, partial purchases, automatic gates and ledger settlement stay native.

The general isolated shore tool also accepts a 0–0.2 m water fringe for
diagnostics. The candidate policy uses zero fringe. Refined probes of the old
v5 snapshot still failed complete quoting; they are not promoted into defenses.
