# Native horde comparison: terminal acceptance review

Source reviewed: `88ebf647b6bbe0d589125e4136d65e73fdf303a6`,
`tools/horde-defense-comparison.mjs`, `tools/horde-defense-farm.mjs`,
`tools/horde-defense-actions.mjs` and `tools/repair-settlement-evidence.mjs`.
This checklist is prepared before results, not evidence of a successful pilot.
The original failed `36a0e204` evidence remains separately preserved.

1. Verify terminal process/session, unchanged source provenance, both original
   native report/state payload hashes and annotated reports. Preserve failures;
   do not rerun or reconstruct missing facts from progress lines.
2. Inspect every returned gate individually. `paired-pilot-terminal` means
   both auditors returned; their booleans may still be false. It is not a
   campaign approval. Twenty nights cannot establish a hundred-night campaign
   or all thirty biome/culture combinations.
3. Compare actual paid staff, wages and physically paid crop deliveries on
   every workday. Keep centre cost 800 and minimum wage 30. Check integer
   ledger entries; fractional maintenance estimates are not settled payments.
4. Review global observed daylight inactivity against the user's strict <25%
   criterion, and disclose opening/final bands and longest idle separately.
   Do not substitute a selected favourable interval or the older fixed
   daily-denominator activity calculation for the observed metric.
5. Inspect native construction payment, actual built wall IDs, gate, skipped
   modules, and surviving/ruined owned structures. A requested wall outline
   does not establish a completed protective enclosure.
6. Separate repair commands from arrival/completion and paid settlement.
   `repairSettlements.status = verified` proves observer coverage and ledger
   reconciliation, even when zero repairs completed. Report requested count,
   completed count, positive paid count, coins and restored HP; inspect pending
   or interrupted work. Stable centre HP alone cannot prove maintenance.
7. Reconcile all native allocated/remaining/spent strikes per raid and actor,
   including worker hit/incapacitation events as well as logical hit/miss.
   Show unused budgets and physical terminal exits; no forced outcome or
   synthetic retirement may stand in for actual native movement.
8. Confirm both arms retain the same productive, magic, staffing and reserve
   policies, with explicit defence/repair differences. Actual outcomes may
   cause divergent crops, RNG histories and income; do not claim paired
   identical attacks. Establish any neglect defeat from native GameOver and
   loss evidence, not an artificial budget penalty or a false summary gate.

If responsible maintenance never completes or neglect survives, report that
result directly. It may guide a subsequent bounded policy or balance change,
but cannot be silently relabelled evidence that defences are necessary.
