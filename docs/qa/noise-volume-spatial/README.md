# Spatial audit of the experimental fine-noise volume

Offline inspection on main `fd48d243`, 7 October 2026. Production shading is
unchanged. The existing QA candidate has a 64³ R8 volume (262,144 source bytes)
and is still outside the gameplay import graph. This complements its prior
[rendered farming measurement](../late-farm-noise-volume/README.md); it does
not repeat that benchmark or establish a new speedup.

`node tools/audit_noise_volume.mjs` models the authored smooth interpolation
and normalized R8 repeat sampling using CPU doubles and the candidate's actual
generated bytes. Each region has 4,096 deterministic points (seed 712).

| Raw scalar error versus analytic noise | Mean absolute | RMS | Maximum |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Positive first tile, excluding wrapped cells | 0.000593 | 0.000726 | 0.001871 |
| Last X cell of the positive tile | 0.118114 | 0.181421 | 0.892301 |
| Negative octant | 0.202983 | 0.256844 | 0.885904 |
| Wide field, X/Z ±1024 and Y ±128 noise coordinates | 0.207532 | 0.262588 | 0.881656 |

Inside unwrapped cells the error stays within half an R8 step, as expected
when all eight corners are shared. Elsewhere the candidate reuses corners from
the first tile: its realization differs much more than quantization alone.
The wrapped field is continuous across tile boundaries; the large last-cell
error is **not a discontinuity**. Negative coordinates are intentionally wrapped
and are not a sampler failure.

Checks pass for the half-byte bound, period, continuity at −64/0/64 and
independent hand-valued interpolation across all three axes, including negative
and wrapped coordinates. Two complete runs produce byte-identical reports:
SHA-256 `3be87b3244ef64e04657fc2b55545f4e8544013cbb42a8be716f4832491ce025`.
The [report](report.json) binds the candidate, recipes and audit tool hashes.

The individual pigment fields repeat along an axis at about 75.29 and 29.09
world units. Their combined weighted scalar pattern has a common axis period
of 1,280 units. The fallback ground's fine field has a 24.62-unit period in
**pattern space**, whose coordinates can be transformed. Other surface textures,
coarse noise, lighting and palettes remain separate: these scalar results do
not prove a visible repeating pattern in the completed shader.

Next acceptance must cover camera paths crossing multiple tiles, negative
coordinates and the world origin, rather than only a close fixed camera. Check
pigment on buildings/actors and fine ground separately, at day/night/intermediate
light; assess repetition perceptually before promoting the candidate. Increasing
volume size alone does not establish matching appearance or better performance.
The candidate remains disabled in gameplay.

This is not GLSL float/hash equivalence, GPU filtering, final shaded-color or
visual acceptance, frametime, mobile, RAM allocation or campaign evidence.
The full regression session was still live during this audit; all 295 hashes
in its captured runtime provenance remained unchanged. This new standalone
tool was not part of that already-started suite.
