Existing historical target.cost is never rewritten. Built wall count does not establish interception or closure.

`createNativeCampaignEvidence` observes each native tick and player decision. Income is exclusively a unique CrateDelivered event matched to its settled deliver:crate ledger entry, not maturity, picking, refunds or estimated payout. It records crop purchases, wall pieces/orders, structure contacts and repair settlements. Paid HP-restoring completions credit only their original request decision; autonomous travel/repair time is not user activity. Missing event windows fail incomplete, including after reload. Day boundary events remain attributed to the outgoing decision. Reports are cloned.

At least60 crop purchases/day is an observed gate, never a quota or free transaction. The harness can attempt one plant each simulated second while time<280, up to280/day, without imposing a plant-count cap. If liquidity, routes or legal plots prevent60, preserve that negative result. A purchase does not represent a whole simulated second of arbitrary UI occupancy: action-based idle remains a policy proxy, not physical human interaction timing.

## Protocol v2: explicit management and exact time/accounting

A / expansive reserves the next already-planned wage, pays pending maintenance, and reinvests remaining funds into native seeds, useful expanding perimeter attempts and new centres; it does not accumulate a village savings target. B / good uses the forecast wage for its living crop load and progressively protects 20% of actual settled crate income, capped at2000, for the next legally permitted village. Each accepted2000 village purchase reduces that cumulative savings target; no escrow is minted or charged. Both can found additional villages only after ordinary postgame continuation. The savings preference yields to the next wage plus one seed when cash is low, so it cannot deliberately lock the last viable planting action. These are player spending preferences, not different prices or wave configurations. Native centre/village previews still decide physical legality. No promise of protection or survival follows from this policy.

C / bad neglects walls, repairs and mid-day hiring while following the same planting species and magic rule. No-walls also omits defenses/repairs but retains mid-day hiring, separating absence of walls from that staffing mistake. All four use the same frozen raids. There is no plant cap, mandatory daily quota, fixed capital200–500, seed grant or fabricated loss. Sixty purchases remains a recorded criterion when resources permit, not a limiter. B can buy affordable partial wall segments while preserving its stated savings preference; an entire perimeter is never a prerequisite to seed investment.

Daily `finance` checkpoints start before the daily contract (day1 also before the first seed) and include every new native journal entry exactly once. `wages` is the actual sum of daily and proportional accepted hire debits, not staff multiplied by a guessed wage. Seeds, walls, centres, villages, settled repairs, delivered-crate income, refunds and uncategorized entries are separately reported; opening+net must equal closing exactly. The centre already paid by createOpeningWorld is outside day1's checkpoint and remains in the retained original state/ledger; it is not silently charged again. Positive unknown entries are disclosed as otherCredits and never called harvest income. The public daily wages includes additional hiring; `additionalWages` is its subset and must not be added again.

Raw idle counts every actual daylight second with no accepted command regardless of reason, including navigation searches, daytime incursions and unknown reasons. It uses native elapsed time clipped to300, including terminal partial days and zero elapsed pending ticks. Meaningful idle remains the stricter observer metric: manual repair requests credit their original decision only after an actual paid HP-restoring settlement; their autonomous task duration is idle. The async driver settles each observer decision with real elapsed time after tick. Both raw and meaningful reports must be retained; acceptance uses meaningful<25%, never a hand-picked subset of idle reasons.

This continuation has not run a native opening or campaign. Pure accounting and observer contracts cannot demonstrate physical routing, earned reserve reachability, wall usefulness or survival. Runtime handshake integration and frozen parent review remain prerequisites to any pilot.
