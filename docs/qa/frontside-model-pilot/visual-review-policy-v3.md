# Visual review policy 3 — explicit user instruction, 8 October 2026

This policy supersedes automatic visual rejection by the pixel, colour, silhouette and small-hole thresholds in the original pilot README for all crops and models. The user explicitly authorized this change. Existing captures, failed thresholds and rejection records remain historical evidence under the policy used when captured; they are not automatically relabelled accepted.

Numeric comparisons remain diagnostics. Record original repeat variation, silhouettes, missing/added pixels, interior regions, linear colour errors, local tiles, maps, shadows and outliers. Exceeding a diagnostic threshold neither stops a new review campaign automatically nor approves or rejects the model. A varying original must be identified in the report, so that its differences are not attributed to a candidate without evidence. Renderer errors, non-finite values, invalid assets, broken contracts and failed cleanup remain failures of functionality or the instrument.

Human inspection in real game conditions decides visual acceptability. Inspect the original and candidate side by side, and alternate them with the same camera, light, growth, wind and animation. At ordinary game distances, the crop must remain recognizable, with its leaves, shape and growth intact, without perceptible deformation, disappearance or defects. Inspect day/night, relevant biomes, underside views, continuous transitions and shadows; diagnostic closeups locate issues but do not by themselves impose mathematical equivalence. Workers need all twelve action clips and their visible tools, body, attachments and shadows, preserving task behavior and skin deformation.

Record the actual reviewer, candidate/source hashes, views and conditions, whether a perceptible defect was found, its evidence and the decision. A fixture emits HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING until this review is recorded; a numeric pass is never a human decision. Representative pilots precede wider category validation. Reusing an old capture for human reevaluation must be labelled retrospective; new captures and continuous real-world inspection are required before category acceptance. No geometry is chosen from per-view visibility masks.

Functional stability, rig/skinning/animations, growth/morphs, UV/material compatibility, topology suitability and reproducibility remain required. Originals stay immutable. Include original/candidate coexistence, bridge buffers, GPU texture fields, draw calls and shadows in resource costs. The existing resource budgets and the measured net GPU gate remain unchanged by this instruction. No candidate inherits the savings of the forced-Front original ceiling experiment. A visually convincing candidate still needs a coordinated net GPU benchmark before integration and a reviewed PR before main changes.

The source-fine field experiment is optional diagnostic work, not a prerequisite for acceptance. Prefer a direct repaired or derived asset if it gives convincing appearance and a net GPU benefit with lower complexity. Exact analytical correspondence and shader-field reconstruction are not required for imperceptible differences.
