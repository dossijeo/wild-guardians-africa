# Ordinary paid defenses in the farming comparison

Run `node tools/check_crop_lifecycle.mjs 20 olderMale OUTPUT_DIRECTORY 8 defend` to compare the higher-staffing strategy with a paid initial farm perimeter. The existing default twelve-plant benchmark and the undefended eight-plant policy are preserved; defense is opt-in.

From day ten, this player policy sets aside 500 coins for walls while keeping ordinary labour and maintenance reserves. It prepares a rectangle around the then-living crops and the actual authored centre footprints, with two metres of clearance. If its nominal full-stroke price exceeds the initial savings target, the target increases instead of perpetually reinvesting the extra money in crops. The production preview rejects unaffordable full strokes, so the policy first saves that nominal upper-bound cost and then asks the production planner for the actual legal modules and price. Construction occurs only when the normal price, next labour reserve, maintenance reserve and already requested repair costs are funded. All pieces, automatic gates and skipped modules come from the production wall planner and normal paid `buildWallChain` command.

After construction, the policy requests ordinary worker repairs of its damaged or ruined wall pieces, keeping funds for existing repair requests. An attack may cancel these tasks normally; the player strategy makes a new manual request during a later peaceful workday. The policy never restores health, rebuilds walls or alters tasks directly.

This is one initial perimeter. Native skipped modules remain gaps, and later plantings can expand outside it without any artificial crop limit. The report records the legal and expected module counts, actual price, IDs, bounds, repair requests and living plants outside the initial perimeter. Those limits must be considered when interpreting losses; this is not a claim of complete adaptive protection for a growing farm.

The native opening check uses the normal 1500 coins, paid centre, eight seeds and one paid older male worker. Ordinary walls are charged, automatic gates remain available, the day reaches dawn and physical crop deliveries still settle in the ledger. A second check rejects unaffordable construction without issuing a command or changing the world. The large-perimeter regression uses two paid, reachable native planting sites and verifies that an insufficient target increases without changing the world or issuing an unfunded command. All three defense checks pass. Ten combined defense, lifecycle and farming checks also passed before adding that regression. The existing complete-state golden hash verifies the original undefended policy is unaffected. Full-length defended profitability, losses and pacing need their own campaign evidence.

The report includes source hashes for this policy module. Wall spending remains a real negative ledger contribution in `otherNet`; the full audit must still reconcile ending cash and every harvested crop. No salary, seed price, growth, damage, FIFO or movement rule is overridden.
