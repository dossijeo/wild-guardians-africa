# Native structure restoration matrix

The original Plan 21.2 saves logical state, not every GPU matrix, particle, or exact animation frame. `tests/browser/structure-restore-world.html` tests actual fresh Navigation/WorldScene reconstruction and original biome chunk streaming. It uses original Sabana terrain, seed 712 and each of the five cultural centre templates. Two legal centres, three legal gates (zarzas/empalizada/reforzado), a millet plant and two older-male workers are paid through production commands. A declared 2000-coin QA credit funds the complete reconstruction scenario; it does not alter the game's 1500 starting balance.

Each culture exercises 14 checkpoints: intact; three native gate families at half and fully open; 40% damage; 78% damage before collapse; 79% collapse start; half collapse; late collapse/ash; ruined; physically rebuilt. Gate requests use the production leaf controller with controlled worker approach positions/routes. Structural hits are directed, then collapse and the worker's repair arrival use real Game.tick. No centre is manually healed. Reconstruction must debit exactly 800 once, and a later tick after reload must not repeat that debit.

Every checkpoint serializes the whole state and loads a fresh navigator and renderer. Damage, holes, shell/interior/ash visibility, scale and gate opening must match. All gate position/normal Float32 arrays are compared directly. The domain snapshot must remain byte-identical after load. Ten checkpoints also stream to a disjoint area 2048 units away and back; resident chunk sets, unchanged domain and restored presentation are checked. Every retired renderer must dispose, lose its WebGL context and terminate its chunk stream.

Transient dust/debris/smoke are safely reconstructed, as Plan 21.2 permits. Their counts before/after are reported separately and are not part of the exact-particle comparison. Previously published spell-duration proof covers all three remaining-duration/cooldown phases, and prior BAST persistence proof covers damage/repair fades and five wall materials. This matrix adds the remaining cultural DEST stages, original open leaves, reconstruction and chunk transitions; it does not claim identical pixels or unchanged decorative particle trajectories.

Results are retained by culture as the directed run completes. Thirty-nine focused building, gate articulation/controller and wall-presentation tests pass. Early fixture development rendered a new scene before loading completed; its own render loop now waits for scene readiness, matching the production application loading boundary. Acceptance records use the completed rerun.
