This refinement preserves that recipe through native `smoothPathSteps`, yielding after four edge checks. Synchronous `smoothPath` drains the same iterator and retains its previous result; the fractional connector delegates cooperatively across its prefix and tail. Collision admission, heuristic and economic/player settings are unchanged.

It was calculated with the old recipe independently of the new methods. Both new interfaces match it exactly: 66 raw tail nodes become five native smoothed tail nodes.
