# Phase 2: post-introduction policy evaluation protocol

The seven-night GA results (`staged-search-v2`, source `7a3df5c4`) are a technical and early-cash screen. Nights 1–5 introduce the raid system, leaving only two meaningful pressured nights in that ranking. Keep its score version `terminal-defeat-v2` immutable; do not use it to select the player policy or infer that walls are unnecessary.

## Versioned next stage

1. Freeze the source and each candidate genome. Use native Wild Engine campaigns of at least 14 nights for the primary search signal, then 21 and 30 nights for finalists. Stop only for native defeat, horizon reached, explicit technical error, or a documented cooperative computational cost limit. A computational stop is censored, never a defeat.
2. Pair each candidate with `good-current-magic` and the same genes with `wallPolicy:'planned'`/`'none'`, holding seed, biome, source revision, agricultural powers and cash policy fixed. Add a no-wall/no-Shield arm where costs allow. Keep legacy profiles as descriptive references, not paired estimates.
3. Report post-intro nights (day 6 onward) separately: delivered crates, exact income, seed/wage/repair/wall expenditure, final cash, live/planted/destroyed crops, raid attraction, structural damage, physical enclosure/breach observations (`unknown` separate), and timing. Compute three-day rolling operating balance from realized income minus paid wages, seeds and repairs, with wall construction shown separately as capital expenditure. Do not infer wall protection from spending alone.
4. Before using any numeric productivity floor, calculate it from frozen native controls on **training** seeds and publish their terminal three-day delivery distribution and ledger. The threshold must represent an economically active farm that can actually pay its workers and seeds, not a chosen number of live plants. Apply the same frozen criterion to untouched biome/seed validation, while showing the raw counts and any false negatives. A one-crop survival with negligible deliveries is a degenerate result even if it lasts to the horizon.
5. Prefer multiple objectives or an explicitly versioned post-intro fitness: native survival, sustained paid production, operating solvency, crop-loss fraction, recovery after a breach, and defensive investment. Keep terminal defeat below a live completion at the same horizon. Do not add a direct penalty for `wallPolicy:'none'`; if it robustly dominates paired defended farms across independent seeds/biomes on survival, deliveries and solvency, report a game-design imbalance instead of a policy success. Fitness weights and reference floors must be committed before a new search and must not be changed during it.

## Current evidence and limits

The Manglares/123 paired 14-night cohort favored walls+Shield strongly, but that seed has been used for development. Across days 6–8 versus 12–14, its delivered crates were 341→352; no-walls+Shield fell 357→203, and no-walls/no-Shield fell 359→157. Final live crops were 167/19/6. The defended arm's final three-day operating balance was nevertheless −142 coins because of ongoing seed purchases and repairs, so a rigid requirement for a positive balance in every short window would wrongly reject an active farm. Calibrate activity from **delivery trends and financial runway together**, not a single cash-flow sign.

Volcanes/907 is censored for the no-wall+Shield arm at day 10 under the earlier headless raid-view bug; all three arms must be rerun at one corrected source hash before comparison. The no-wall seven-night leader lost **cumulatively** 50 crops in Sabana/411 (23 on day 6, 27 on day 7), 51 in Gran Río/626 (25+26), and 53 in Desierto/431 (3+3 on days 3–4 and 21+26 on days 6–7), while the short score favored its cash. This illustrates why the primary fitness must cover more raids and recovery.

No 14/21-night evolutionary search or 100-night victory is claimed here. The military/economic balance of production remains unchanged.
