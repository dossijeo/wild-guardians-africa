# Volcanes/907 native raid entry at day 10

The matched no-walls/current-magic campaign failed with `Native raid entry failed` at day 10, time 355. The compressed failure artifact is preserved as [`tests/fixtures/volcanes-907-day10-native-entry.json.gz`](../../../tests/fixtures/volcanes-907-day10-native-entry.json.gz). It came from `docs/qa/evolutionary-balance/matched-defense-volcanes-14/results/matched-no-walls-current-magic-volcanes-907-14.json.gz` in the evolutionary-balance lab, source SHA `7fd6c1bcb0c725da7b8449bbb2514859eb5a92f1`; the copied gzip has SHA-256 `9feb237d1f7638b1cba1d68b34ec10bc98810c7ccaabcee14924867d827c1969`.

The saved state has a live replacement center `structure-2` at `(83, 0)` and a planned raid of 15 animals. The headless runner's raid camera was still aimed at `(84, 9)`, with eye `(100.70247532024266, 39.57367599590944)`. With the same state, native terrain, loaded chunks, and raid group, that stale view produces `raidEntryFailure`. Recomputing the camera from the live center allows all 15 animals to enter with walkable origins and clear exits. The engine's native spawn rules and damage were not changed.

The continuous campaign exposed the missing step in the first fix: `native-campaign-plots.mjs::focus()` also writes `raidView` while searching parcels. The center was unchanged, so a center-only sync left this plot view active. The runner now restores its expected raid view after decisions and ticks whenever it differs, and updates active bounds only with that restoration or a center change. It leaves bounds expanded for an already admitted raid untouched. Additional hiring targets the live center; the old cached opening-center reference was invalid after replacement. The focused native regression restores the failure artifact, invokes the real plot-search focus path to overwrite the view, confirms the stale-view failure, then verifies successful physical entry after restoration.

A microbenchmark on 14 centers with the only live center last measured approximately 0.003 ms for three center scans across 50 ticks and 0.002 ms for two scans (warm V8, 30,000 repetitions). The redundant pre-presentation scan was removed; the remaining checks protect center changes caused by decisions and by ticks.
