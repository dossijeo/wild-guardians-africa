bytes (73.688 MiB), including future and splice windows. Peak scheduled music
sources is 32; this includes queued future sources and is not the number of
simultaneously audible instruments. It is not total browser RAM.

Global/family/emitter SFX limits pass throughout (sampled peak six SFX), with
no music errors, unexpected transport restarts or WebGL errors. B starts
without changing the paused hiring snapshot. The native wind source survives
the pack change; the A pool is disposed with zero retained PCM. Stop leaves

This verifies first-day 3D integration and A-to-B dawn after a real incursion.
