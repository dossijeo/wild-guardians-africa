# Volcanoes geometry residency diagnostic

Run `node tools/experiments/audit-volcano-geometry-lifetime-20261022.mjs`, then `python tools/experiments/summarize-volcano-geometry-lifetime-20261022.py`.

Same saved populated Volcanoes fixture, seed 712, full background, fixed normal camera, early simulation pause, medium/low/medium/low, day/night. QA-only instrumentation observes Three's onGeometryDispose listener registration/removal to enumerate GPU-resident geometries. Every snapshot asserts that the census count matches renderer.info.memory.geometries. Snapshot after 90 stable frames and again after 180 additional frames. No production imports or rendering changes; these runs are not timing evidence.

All eight records are error-free. Both medium runs have 334 resident geometries. Low changes from 308 to 309 on the final repetition: the extra geometry belongs to Scene/native_merged_assets/native-obstruction-blend, with 2216 vertices, 2874 indices and a one-element nativeVisibility attribute. It is visible at the day snapshot and hidden at night, but stays GPU-resident. No additions/removals occur during the 180-frame observation intervals. Medium recorded descriptors match (type, index and attribute counts, parameters, scene owner paths and local positions). This does not hash vertex contents or prove universal resource ownership; unreferenced geometries can belong to auxiliary scenes or caches.

The earlier benchmark's 335-versus-334 medium discrepancy did NOT recur in medium, but the same one-geometry count variation appeared in low and is now localized to the obstruction blend geometry. This is evidence of a specific allocation-history candidate, not proof of the original run's cause. Do not retrospectively accept that benchmark, claim a leak is fixed, or infer performance from this diagnostic. Ground mask preupload was not applied here, so texture counts are not controlled benchmark evidence. Keep the earlier strict failure and original data. Inspect obstruction-blend initialization and establish repeatable preparation before a fresh uninstrumented benchmark; broader active-play and streaming validation remains outstanding.

Artistic selections remain unchanged, including the user's original dense Mangrove leaves (left side of the leaf-treatment comparison).

Source inspection: NativeAssetGroups.prepare creates a one-instance geometry view lazily when visibility is between .002 and .999. Unused smoothFades are hidden, not disposed; retire disposes them when the owning group is retired. Therefore resource residency can retain a prior obstruction transition even when the final draw workload matches. This explains how the observed low-profile difference can persist, without establishing which initial camera/loading event created it. Do not replace smooth transparency with dithering or remove its geometry to make the audit pass.
