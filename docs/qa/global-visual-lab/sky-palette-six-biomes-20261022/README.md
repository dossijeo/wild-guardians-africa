# Environment palette review across six biomes

72 real captures /36 matched pairs:seed712,media,1280x720,game/elevated/reverse views,day/night. Both variants have cloud-protected sky grading,subtle cloud latitude and matched reflection direction. Only candidate adds worker-precomputed environment palette. All paired camera,target,time,draws,triangles match; no recorded errors. Candidate worker metadata recorded. Desert retains sandMeso=1. Reproduce with capture-sky-palette-six-biomes-20261022.mjs and package-sky-palette-six-biomes-20261022.py,Vite5190. Uses V11 material/coverage QA adapters,not a finished unified preset.

Inspected all12 paired sheets. Sabana/Grand River matte ground and vegetation remain readable,with small reflection changes. Mangrove buildings retain orange warmth; moss character retained. Volcano material becomes slightly cooler in reflective areas while warm buildings/lava remain distinct. Approved Desert relief/shadows and golden color retained. Canyon is the important exception:full palette shifts broad water toward stronger blue and reduces its softer turquoise appearance. Near white reflection patches look more isolated. This is not a uniformly better global direction.

Decision: do NOT adopt full environment recoloring globally. Preserve the worker palette candidate for optional wet-biome art review,not discard it solely on performance. Integrate cloud placement and matched reflection direction with the original environment radiance palette. Artistic consistency does not require identical display/BRDF tone curves; the authored water palette takes precedence over mathematical display-color matching. Benefits of grading are too small on matte surfaces to justify this water change everywhere.

These are visual/counter comparisons,not new timing benchmarks. Earlier per-frame and one-time build evidence remains in sky-environment-palette-cost-20261022 and sky-environment-palette-20261022. Global quality and low-profile/motion validation still incomplete.
