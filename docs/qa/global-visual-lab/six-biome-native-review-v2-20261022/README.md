Art assessment:

- River: filtering allows geometric cover to read above underlying texture more clearly. Still too uniform at larger scales and broad bare paths are abrupt; do not call grass appearance finished.
- Mangroves: village camera remains mostly moss, with little visible water. Use the separately inspected bank-native-parity series to judge shoreline improvement. Still lacks reference-like irregular banks and layered wetland composition; more global density is not automatically the answer.
- Savanna: warm continuous cover and readable shadows, but reference has broader clumped accumulation and less uniform small detail. Preserve silhouettes.
- Canyon: warm strata are strong, but broad blue river and smooth dark banks remain the largest water/material gap. Previous subtle bank trials did not solve it.
- Volcanoes: rock relief is visible but remains cold gray across broad open ground; reference has warm sunlit ridges and richer local dry cover. Next bounded experiment: warmer direct ground-light response preserving night/ambient and all models, evaluated against matched current frames.
- Desert: retain the approved warm relief/shadows. Do not retune it merely because other biomes need work; small detail distribution is a separate future topic.

Recommendation: retain native River/Mangrove additions as explicit options, continue artistic work. Next Volcano ground sunlight is a bounded remaining gap; Canyon water needs a more meaningful depth/shore approach after that. Prior subsystem benchmarks quantify costs but this review is not a full-scene performance validation. Additional seeds, modest quality, live campaigns and fast streaming remain broader requirements. The global goal is not achieved and main must not be merged on this evidence.
