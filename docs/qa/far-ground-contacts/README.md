# Regional terrain contacts and ground color

Experimental Sabana layer only. Billboards preserve native procedural position, yaw and anisotropic scale. The worker inserts each authored foot as a contact vertex into the coarse ground triangulation, including both triangles/cells when the point lies on an edge. It interpolates existing colors and does not resample procedural noise per contact. Triangle orientation and nonzero area are retained. Duplicate/outside contact positions do not allocate unused vertices.

Controlled seed 712 region centered at [76.70247532024266,30.57367599590944], tree half extent 400, ground half extent 432, step 8: 651 trees/651 contact vertices. Maximum vertical error at float32 billboard foot coordinates reduces from 0.6679693445533417 to 4.757651073106217e-7 units. Procedural tree arrays are exactly unchanged. Ground triangles increase from 23328 to 24630 (5.6%) at identical bounds. The adapter also expands its former 400 ground half extent to 432 to cover offset authored feet, so its total ground triangles increase by approximately 23% versus the previous 20000-triangle proxy. This is additional GPU geometry; no free frametime or performance improvement is claimed. Contact work runs in the regional worker, never per gameplay frame.

The proxy was also entering SceneMaterialRegistry and receiving the full toon recipe. It is now explicitly excluded, uses an unlit vertex-color material with fog, and treats the washed procedural palette as display sRGB converted to linear once during preparation. Tone mapping is disabled to prevent an additional grading of that palette. Position/color source arrays remain unchanged. This removes the inappropriate complex surface shader from the far proxy. Day/night tint matching remains pending.

Real-world QA: medium Sabana, seed 712, Mapungubwe, 25 chunks, 646 regional trees, 18 ready nearby native trees, one native GPU preparation, no runtime/WebGL errors. Screenshot retained. ANGLE environment4 warnings, if present, are preserved in console.json. Visual support and far ground colors improve, but all-angle transitions, native LOD2 atlas matching, horizon composition, night appearance and GPU/mobile measurements are still pending. The layer remains disabled in ordinary games.

25 focused tests pass, including authored contacts, cell/diagonal boundaries, duplicates, original terrain behavior, seeded correspondence, clipping, resource ownership, worker transfer and native coverage. No full-goal completion claim.
